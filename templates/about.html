{{ icon('user','icon-sm') }} Who We Are

A SMALL TEAM.
BIG AMBITIONS.

IronTrack is an independent project created by a small team of lifters, runners and engineers. We started it because we were tired of fitness apps that charge for basic features, sell your data and clutter every screen with ads.

So we built the tracker we wanted: free, fast and focused. What began as a simple gym log has grown into a full platform with training plans, progress history, health calculators and an AI coaching assistant. And it stays free for everyone.
